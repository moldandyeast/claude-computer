CC3

A computer you and Claude share. Claude answers here, with files and small apps you can both use, change and keep.

  • Talk to Claude in the chat and watch it build here: documents, spreadsheets, slides, canvases and small apps. The cube flies to whatever Claude is working on.
  • Press / to open the launcher and make anything new, or find what is here. F fills the screen with the selected app; Esc comes back.
  • Double-click anywhere to write a note, or type below. Everything you change, Claude sees on its next turn.
  • Share it from the Share menu to work on it together: everyone's pointer, selection and Claude show up here, live, and each person keeps their own answers in polls, quizzes and forms.
